Essential Travel Information

Everything you need to know before and during your visit to Taniti. From currency and power outlets to local regulations and safety tips.

💵

Currency & Payment

Accepted Currency

  • Primary: U.S. dollars (USD)
  • Also accepted: Euros (EUR) and Japanese Yen (JPY) at many establishments
  • Credit cards: Visa, Mastercard, and American Express widely accepted

ATMs & Banking

  • ATMs available in Taniti City and at the airport
  • Most ATMs dispense U.S. dollars
  • Banks open Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 4 PM
  • Currency exchange available at airport and major hotels

Tipping

  • Not required but appreciated (10-15% is customary)
  • Service charges sometimes included in bills
🔌

Power & Electronics

Electrical Standards

  • Voltage: 120 volts (same as United States)
  • Frequency: 60 Hz
  • Plug type: Type A and Type B (American-style)

What This Means

  • U.S. and Canadian devices work without adapters
  • European and UK visitors need voltage converters and plug adapters
  • Most modern phone/laptop chargers are dual voltage (check your device)

Connectivity

  • Free Wi-Fi at most hotels, restaurants, and cafés
  • Mobile coverage available island-wide
  • International roaming rates may apply
🛡️

Safety & Health

General Safety

  • Crime level: Low - Violent crime is very rare
  • Be aware: Petty theft and pickpocketing can occur in crowded tourist areas
  • Tips: Keep valuables secured; use hotel safes; stay aware of surroundings
  • Emergency services: Dial 911 (police, fire, ambulance)

Healthcare Facilities

  • One full-service hospital in Taniti City
  • Several medical clinics across the island
  • Multilingual staff available
  • Travel insurance recommended
  • Pharmacy services available

Water & Food Safety

  • Tap water is safe to drink
  • Restaurants follow health and safety standards
  • Fresh seafood is inspected daily
📋

Local Regulations

Local regulations are laws and rules specific to Taniti that visitors should be aware of to ensure a safe and respectful visit.

Alcohol Laws

  • Sales hours: Alcohol cannot be sold or served between midnight and 9:00 AM
  • Legal drinking age: 18 years old
  • Public consumption: Allowed on beaches but not in parks or city streets
  • ID may be requested at bars and restaurants

Transportation Rules

  • Bicycle helmets: Required by law for all riders
  • Driver's license: Valid license from home country accepted for up to 90 days
  • Seatbelts: Mandatory for all passengers
  • Speed limits: Posted in mph; strictly enforced

Environmental Protection

  • No removal of coral, shells, or volcanic rocks
  • Stay on marked trails in rainforest and volcano areas
  • Littering carries heavy fines
  • Respect wildlife - no feeding or touching

Cultural Etiquette

  • Modest dress required when visiting religious sites
  • Photography restrictions at some cultural sites (signage posted)
  • Tipping not mandatory but appreciated
🗣️

Language & Communication

Official Language

  • Primary: Tanitian (local language)
  • Second language: English widely spoken

English Proficiency

  • Tourist areas: Excellent - English is common in Taniti City, resorts, and attractions
  • Young residents: Most speak English fluently
  • Rural areas: Limited English; basic phrases helpful
  • Hotel and restaurant staff are multilingual

Helpful Phrases

  • Hello - "Mālō" (MAH-loh)
  • Thank you - "Fa'afetai" (fah-ah-FEH-tie)
  • Please - "Fa'amolemole" (fah-ah-moh-leh-MOH-leh)
🎉

National Holidays & Events

Important Note

  • Some attractions, restaurants, and shops close on national holidays
  • Plan ahead and check opening hours during holiday periods
  • Hotels and resorts remain open year-round

Major Holidays

  • Independence Day: June 15 (biggest celebration)
  • Founder's Day: September 1
  • Harvest Festival: Mid-November
  • International holidays: Christmas, New Year's Day

Peak Travel Seasons

  • High season: December-April (dry season, ideal weather)
  • Shoulder season: May-June, November (good weather, fewer crowds)
  • Low season: July-October (wetter weather, best deals)

Quick Reference

Emergency: 911
Time Zone: UTC-11
Driving: Right side of road
Drinking Age: 18 years
Voltage: 120V / 60Hz
Currency: USD, EUR, JPY
