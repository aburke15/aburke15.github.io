Lodging Options on Taniti

From budget-friendly hostels to luxury resorts, Taniti offers a variety of accommodations to suit every traveler's needs and budget. All lodging is regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.

Four-Star Resort

Luxury

Experience ultimate relaxation at Taniti's premier beachfront resort. Features include:

  • Oceanfront rooms with private balconies
  • Full-service spa and fitness center
  • Multiple on-site restaurants and bars
  • Private beach access
  • Complimentary transportation service
  • Concierge and tour booking services

Transportation: Resort offers complimentary shuttle service to major attractions and the airport.

Family-Owned Hotels

Mid-Range

Enjoy personalized service and local hospitality at one of Taniti's charming family-run hotels:

  • Comfortable rooms with modern amenities
  • Locations in Taniti City and near Yellow Leaf Bay
  • Local cuisine at on-site restaurants
  • Helpful staff with insider knowledge
  • Bicycle rentals available
  • Some offer airport pickup service

Transportation: Most hotels can arrange taxi service or car rentals. Some offer complimentary airport pickup.

Bed & Breakfasts

Mid-Range

Experience authentic island living in cozy bed and breakfast accommodations:

  • Intimate settings with 3-8 guest rooms
  • Home-cooked breakfast included
  • Located in residential neighborhoods
  • Personal attention from hosts
  • Unique island decor and architecture
  • Garden or patio common areas

Transportation: Hosts can help arrange rental cars or connect you with local taxi services.

Private Rentals

Mid-Range

Rent an entire home or apartment for privacy and flexibility:

  • Fully equipped kitchens
  • Living spaces for families or groups
  • Beachfront and mountain view options
  • Weekly and monthly rates available
  • Private parking included
  • Self-catering convenience

Transportation: Rental car highly recommended for private rentals. Parking included with all properties.

Hostel

Budget

Perfect for budget-conscious travelers and backpackers:

  • Dormitory and private room options
  • Shared kitchen facilities
  • Common lounge and social areas
  • Located in central Taniti City
  • Walking distance to beaches and attractions
  • Organized group activities and tours

Transportation: Walkable to most city attractions. Bicycle rentals and bus passes available at reception.
