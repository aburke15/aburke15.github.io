Dining on Taniti

Experience authentic island flavors and international cuisine. From fresh seafood to traditional Tanitian dishes, there's something to satisfy every palate and budget.

🍽️ Dining Overview

Taniti offers 10 restaurants serving a variety of cuisines. Five restaurants specialize in local fish and traditional Tanitian cuisine, while others offer American, Italian, and Pan-Asian fare. All restaurants welcome families and accommodate dietary restrictions with advance notice.

🐟

Fresh Seafood & Local Cuisine

5 restaurants specializing in island flavors

  • Catch of the day - locally sourced fish
  • Traditional Tanitian dishes
  • Beachfront and waterfront locations
  • Sunset dining available
  • Family-friendly atmosphere

Popular dishes: Grilled mahi-mahi, coconut curry, island poke bowls

🍕

American & Italian Cuisine

Comfort food and international favorites

  • Pizza, pasta, and burgers
  • Steaks and grilled specialties
  • Vegetarian options available
  • Family-style portions
  • Kid-friendly menus

Best for: Families with children, casual dining, familiar favorites

🍜

Pan-Asian Dining

Fusion and traditional Asian cuisine

  • Sushi and sashimi
  • Ramen, pho, and noodle dishes
  • Stir-fries and curries
  • Fresh ingredients daily
  • Vegetarian and vegan options

Popular choices: Sushi platters, pad thai, ramen bowls

☕

Coffee Shops & Cafés

Light bites and refreshments

  • Locally roasted coffee
  • Fresh pastries and sandwiches
  • Smoothies and fresh juices
  • Breakfast all day
  • Free Wi-Fi available

Perfect for: Morning coffee, quick lunch, casual meetings

🍹

Bars & Pubs

Evening drinks and light fare

  • Local microbrewery with craft beers
  • Tropical cocktails and island specialties
  • Pub food and appetizers
  • Live music on weekends
  • Happy hour specials

Note: Alcohol not served midnight-9 AM. Drinking age is 18.

🥘

Grocery Stores & Markets

Self-catering options

  • Two supermarkets in Taniti City
  • Fresh produce and local products
  • International food selection
  • Deli and prepared foods
  • Perfect for private rental guests

Ideal for: Private rental guests, picnic supplies, snacks

Important Dining Information

💰 Payment Options

  • U.S. dollars accepted everywhere
  • Many establishments accept euros and yen
  • Major credit cards widely accepted
  • ATMs available in Taniti City

🕐 Dining Hours

  • Most restaurants: 11 AM - 10 PM
  • Breakfast cafés: 6 AM - 11 AM
  • Bars: Open until midnight
  • Some locations close on national holidays

🥗 Dietary Accommodations

  • Vegetarian options at all restaurants
  • Vegan options available (ask server)
  • Gluten-free requests honored with notice
  • Allergy information provided upon request

📍 Locations

  • Most restaurants in Taniti City
  • Beachfront dining at Yellow Leaf Bay
  • Resort restaurants open to public
  • Merriton Landing has several options
