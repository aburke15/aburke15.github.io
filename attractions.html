Attractions on Taniti

Discover the natural beauty and exciting activities that make Taniti a premier tropical destination. From pristine beaches to volcanic adventures, there's something for everyone!

⭐ Most Popular Attractions

Based on visitor surveys - these are the top experiences visitors come to Taniti for!

🏖️

Sandy Beaches at Yellow Leaf Bay

Pristine white sand beaches perfect for swimming, sunbathing, and water sports. The most photographed location on the island.

Top Rated
🌋

Active Volcano Tours

Guided tours to Taniti's active volcano. Safe viewing platforms offer spectacular views of volcanic activity and the island.

Top Rated
🌳

Rainforest Hiking Trails

Explore lush tropical rainforest with trails for all skill levels. Spot exotic wildlife and discover hidden waterfalls.

Top Rated
🤿

Snorkeling & Diving

Crystal-clear waters teeming with marine life. Guided snorkeling and scuba diving excursions available for all experience levels.

Top Rated
🎣

Chartered Fishing Tours

Deep-sea fishing adventures for experienced anglers and beginners. Equipment and instruction provided.

Top Rated
🌅

Beachfront Restaurants

Dine with your toes in the sand while watching stunning sunsets. Fresh seafood and traditional Tanitian cuisine.
