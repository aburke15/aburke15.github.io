Beaches, rainforest, and an active volcano

Taniti is a small tropical island with varied terrain: sandy and rocky beaches, lush rainforests, and a mountainous interior with a small active volcano.

Easy stays for couples and small groups

Visitor survey data shows many trips are taken by pairs, with hotels as the most common lodging choice. Taniti also offers bed and breakfasts, private rentals, and one large four-star resort.

Arrive by air and get around with ease

Most visitors arrive by air, with some arriving by cruise ship. On the island, rental cars are common, and Taniti City is flat and walkable. Public buses operate daily from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m.

Popular attractions

Most visitors come for beaches, rainforest, and the volcano. Many activities are near Taniti City and Merriton Landing.

  • Beaches around Yellow Leaf Bay and Taniti City
  • Rainforest hikes, snorkeling, zip-lining
  • Visits to Taniti's active volcano
  • Local history museum, art galleries, movie theater, arcade, bowling
  • Chartered fishing tours, helicopter rides, pubs and a microbrewery, dance club

Lodging options

Hotels are the most common choice among surveyed visitors, with options ranging from budget to resort stays.

  • Inexpensive hostel
  • Family-owned hotels
  • Bed and breakfasts
  • Private rentals
  • One large four-star resort

All lodging is regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.

Transportation info

  • Arrivals: small airport (expanding for larger jets); cruise ship docks one night per week
  • Taniti City buses: 5 a.m. to 11 p.m., daily
  • Private buses serve the rest of the island
  • Taxis available in Taniti City
  • Rental cars near the airport (common choice among surveyed visitors)
  • Bikes and helmets for rent (helmets required)

Taniti City is fairly flat and walkable. Merriton Landing is also easy to explore on foot.

Practical travel info

  • Power: 120 volts (same as the United States)
  • Currency: U.S. dollars; many businesses accept euros and yen; major credit cards accepted
  • Alcohol: Not sold or served from midnight to 9:00 a.m.; drinking age is 18
  • Language: English is common among younger residents; rural areas have limited English
  • Safety: Violent crime is rare, but pickpocketing and petty crime may occur
  • Healthcare: One hospital and several clinics, with multilingual employees
  • Holidays: Some attractions and restaurants may close on national holidays
