Transportation on Taniti

Getting around Taniti is easy! Choose from various transportation options depending on your lodging and travel preferences.

Transportation by Lodging Type

🏨 Four-Star Resort

Included Transportation: The resort offers complimentary shuttle service to the airport, Taniti City, and major attractions. No additional transport needed for most guests!

🏠 Hotels, B&Bs, and Private Rentals

Recommended: Rental car for maximum flexibility. Many properties offer free parking. Some hotels provide airport pickup service.

🎒 Hostel

Options: Walking distance to city attractions. Public buses, bicycle rentals, and occasional taxi service are perfect for hostel guests on a budget.

Available Transportation Options

🚗 Rental Cars

Most popular option among visitors. Available near the airport and in Taniti City.

  • Wide selection of vehicles
  • Daily and weekly rates
  • Valid driver's license required
  • GPS available upon request

🚌 Public Buses

Affordable way to get around Taniti City and nearby areas.

  • Taniti City: 5 AM - 11 PM daily
  • Private buses serve the rest of the island
  • Multi-day passes available
  • Schedules available at hotels

🚕 Taxis

Convenient for short trips and airport transfers.

  • Available in Taniti City
  • Can be arranged through hotels
  • Metered and flat-rate options
  • Credit cards accepted

🚲 Bicycle Rentals

Perfect for exploring flat, walkable areas like Taniti City.

  • Helmets required by law (included)
  • Hourly and daily rates
  • Available near beaches and hotels
  • Great for short distances
