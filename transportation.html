Transportation on Taniti

Getting around Taniti is easy! Most visitors arrive at Taniti Airport. Choose from multiple transportation options to reach your destination and explore the island.

🛬 From the Airport

First-time visitors: Here are your options for getting from Taniti Airport to your accommodation.

🚗 Rental Cars At Airport

Most popular choice among visitors. Pick up your rental directly from the airport.

  • Multiple rental companies at airport
  • Wide selection of vehicles
  • Daily and weekly rates available
  • Valid driver's license required
  • Free parking at most hotels and B&Bs

Best for: All lodging types except the Four-Star Resort (which includes shuttle service)

🚕 Taxis At Airport

Convenient door-to-door service. Available at the airport taxi stand.

  • Direct service to any accommodation
  • Flat-rate fares to major areas
  • Credit cards accepted
  • No reservation needed
  • Approximately 20-30 minute ride to Taniti City

Best for: Hostel guests, those staying short-term, or visitors who don't plan to travel around the island frequently

🚌 Public Buses At Airport

Most affordable option. Regular service connects the airport to Taniti City.

  • Bus stop located outside airport terminal
  • Runs every 30-45 minutes
  • Service hours: 5 AM - 11 PM daily
  • Multi-day passes available
  • Stops at major hotels and city center

Best for: Budget travelers, hostel guests, and those staying in Taniti City

🚐 Resort Shuttle At Airport

Complimentary for resort guests. Pre-arranged pickup service.

  • Free shuttle service to/from airport
  • Reservation required (provide flight details)
  • Professional drivers with signage
  • Also provides transportation to attractions
  • Available 24/7 for guest arrivals

Available for: Four-Star Resort guests only (included in stay)

🚖 Hotel Pickup Service

Arranged through your accommodation. Some hotels offer airport transfers.

  • Available at select family-owned hotels
  • May be complimentary or for a fee
  • Advance reservation required
  • Contact your hotel directly to arrange
  • Confirm availability when booking lodging

Available for: Some family-owned hotels and B&Bs (inquire when booking)

🏝️ Getting Around the Island

Once you've reached your accommodation, here are your options for exploring Taniti.

🚗 Rental Cars (Island-Wide)

Maximum flexibility to explore at your own pace.

  • Available at airport and in Taniti City
  • GPS available upon request
  • Easy access to beaches, volcano, and rainforest
  • Free parking at most attractions
  • Well-maintained roads throughout island

Ideal for: Hotels, B&Bs, private rentals, and hostels

🚌 Public Buses (City & Island)

Affordable and eco-friendly way to travel.

  • Taniti City buses: 5 AM - 11 PM daily
  • Private buses serve rest of island
  • Multi-day passes save money
  • Schedules available at hotels and bus stops
  • Stops at major attractions and beaches

Ideal for: Budget travelers, hostel guests, and environmentally conscious visitors

🚕 Taxis (On-Demand)

Convenient for specific trips and outings.

  • Available throughout Taniti City
  • Can be arranged through any hotel
  • Metered and flat-rate options
  • Credit cards accepted
  • Ideal for evening dining and nightlife

Ideal for: All lodging types for occasional trips

🚲 Bicycle Rentals (Local Exploration)

Perfect for short distances and beach areas.

  • Helmets required by law (included)
  • Hourly and daily rates
  • Available near beaches, hotels, and city center
  • Great for flat areas like Taniti City
  • Eco-friendly way to see the sights

Ideal for: All lodging types for local exploration

🚶 Walking (City Center)

Taniti City and Merriton Landing are pedestrian-friendly.

  • Taniti City is flat and easy to navigate on foot
  • Many attractions within walking distance
  • Sidewalks and crosswalks well-maintained
  • No special equipment needed
  • Free and healthy way to explore

Ideal for: Hostel guests and those staying in city center
